About Us

A family-owned publishing house since 1998

Baldwin Terney Press is a small family-owned book publishing company started in 1998 in the USA. We believe that literature allows our imagination to take flight, teaches us invaluable lessons, and steers our lives. We are proud publishers of imaginative illustrators, gifted storytellers, and knowledgeable instructors.

Authors

Books for kids. Insight for buyers.

Baldwin Terney Press started as a family project — a place to publish stories for the kids we love. It's grown into three things at once: picture books that spark imagination; Cyber Elementary School, a 36-lesson cyber security, online safety, and AI safety curriculum for grades 3–6; and the Managed Security Buyers Guide News series — a weekly, plain-spoken briefing for the people who buy managed security services, drawn from 25+ years in cyber operations and building managed security programs for critical infrastructure. All three share the same goal: cut through the noise and say something useful.

James Mattei

James Mattei is the author of The "COMP"rehensive guide to the Gulf Coast and The "COMP"rehensive guide to Atlantic City. He is a retired probation officer who lives on Long Island, New York. He has a BS in Mathematics from Manhattan College and a Law Degree from Blackstone School of Law. During his time on Long Island he was the Mayor of Manorhaven for 10 years. He is married to his lovely wife, Marion, and has two sons and five grandchildren. He spends his time traveling when he is not writing about gaming and the best way to get the most out of your money.

Rachel Mattei

Rachel Mattei is the writer and illustrator of I Need Chocolate Milk! and I Need Strawberry Milk! But more importantly, she is the proud mother of two very imaginative and lovable children, who constantly require (you guessed it) chocolate milk. When she is not writing and drawing, she is also drinking chocolate milk with them. Rachel was an Army brat and married an Army officer, so she has lived all over the world, including Germany, Kuwait, and the United States. She currently lives with her husband and children in Phoenix, Arizona.

Mark Mattei

Mark Mattei is the owner of Baldwin Terney Press, creator of Cyber Elementary, and author of the Managed Security Buyers Guide News series. He is from Long Island, New York and spent 22 years in the Army as an officer. He has a background in telecommunications and cyber security. He currently lives in Phoenix, Arizona with his wife and two children.
